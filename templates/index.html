Compliance Checklist

Small & Manageable steps towards better security
Illustrative Checklist
Review Employee Access Rights
Check who can access practice systems and patient information. Confirm that permissions are appropriate for each person's role.
Access Management Not completed
▤
What To Do

Review current user accounts and their permissions. Remove access that is no longer needed and document when the review took place and what measures have been taken.

Example Evidence

A dated access-review record. Avoid including passwords or unnecessary patient information.

Check Backups & Recovery
Review whether important practice data is backed up and whether there is a documented process for restoring it if systems fail.
Business Continuity Not completed
▤
What To Do

Confirm that backups are running, check who is responsible for them and review whether restoration has been tested.

Example Evidence

A dated backup-review checklist or a record of a successful restoration test.

Document Security Awareness Training
Keep track of relevant security guidance provided to staff, such as recognising phishing attempts and handling sensitive information.
People & Awareness Not completed
▤
What To Do

Identify relevant training needs and record what training was provided, when it took place and who participated.

Example evidence

A dated training log or attendance record, stored securely with access restricted to authorised personnel.

Understand what your records can demonstrate
Keeping dated evidence can help a practice explain its security activities. Cryptographic hashes recorded on Solana could help verify whether evidence matches a version committed earlier. A matching hash does not, by itself, establish that a practice was legally compliant. This demonstration does not validate legal requirements.
